The next one is some kind of internal or embedded evaluators. As you guys know very well, the Hugging Face incident with OpenAI happened during testing, so it was not a released system. We've gotta guard against those kind of risks as well and make sure the companies are living up to their standards there. The third is some kind of cybersecurity standard to guard against foreign espionage. This was a hypothetical threat. Now we've got a lot of evidence that the spies are after American companies. Part of, as I said, these companies growing up in the private sector, this technology growing up in the private sector, is they're not alert to the means of foreign government. So what are the cybersecurity and maybe even personnel security standards that the government should require of the companies and also help the companies with? And the fourth one is I'm an advocate for pacing the frontier, and I'm an advocate for slowing down, and I think there is a role for the government in figuring out what that looks like to slow down companies and make sure they're not racing ahead and racing to the bottom. So that, I think, is probably enough for the Congress to get going with in 2027, and I hope they're ready to get to work.
